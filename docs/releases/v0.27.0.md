# Axiom v0.27.0

Version `0.27.0` is the measured follow-up to Issue #253. Re-observing the immutable v0.26.0 in workspaces outside any instruction chain (Issue #251) showed that the schema v8 overlap case, a request to redesign a packaged plugin's shared routing architecture and explicitly measure the resulting context cost, selected `optimize-agent-usage` beside `agent-plugin-architect` in a minority of clean-location trials on both hosts; [Routing Evaluations](https://github.com/wheakerd/axiom/blob/v0.27.0/evals/README.md#contract-changes) records the counts and their composition. The `agent-plugin-architect` skill already states that an explicit usage-cost goal may add `optimize-agent-usage`, so the two-route expectation is the design and the selection wording was the defect. This release names an explicit goal to measure or reduce context cost in the `optimize-agent-usage` description, keeps the startup gate unchanged, and records the two-arm measurement behind it.

## Why A Minor

The [release standard](../runtime-identity.md#version-increment) assigns a minor increment when matching widens to requests a route did not select before. The usage route now selects on an explicit measurement goal that the 0.26.0 wording left to chance.

## What Changed

- Description: the `optimize-agent-usage` limiting sentence reads "Use only on an explicit goal to measure or reduce context cost, save credits or tokens, trim Skills, instruction files, MCP, or context loading, explain unusually high usage, or design a leaner workflow." The first and last sentences are unchanged.
- Gate: unchanged. A first candidate also reworded the gate's usage sentence; the two-arm measurement below rejected it, and the description alone carries the change.
- Corpus and benchmarks: unchanged; routing contract v8 and both 49-case host profiles keep their cases and expectations.
- Documentation: [Routing Evaluations](https://github.com/wheakerd/axiom/blob/v0.27.0/evals/README.md) records the decision of Issue #253 with its counts.

## Two-Arm Measurement

Every session ran fresh and isolated in a workspace outside any instruction chain, with the benchmark developer instruction and response schema V8, arm A the immutable v0.26.0 plugin tree and arm B this candidate, Claude Code 2.1.309 with `claude-opus-5-5` and Codex CLI 0.166.0 with `gpt-6-astra`, both at maximum effort, on 2026-11-04.

1. Stage 1, eight targeted cases, four trials per arm per host: the overlap case routed both routes under A in 1 of 4 Claude Code and 3 of 4 Codex trials, under B in 4 of 4 on each host; the two other usage positives, the two architect positives, the phase case, the performance control, and the no-route control kept their route sets in every trial under both arms.
2. Stage 2, the 49 benchmark cases and the 18 corpus forms of 0.26.0, one trial per arm per host, under the 0.26.0 decision rule: Claude Code routed 41 of 42 under A and 42 of 42 under B with 25 of 25 controls host-native under both; Codex routed 40 of 42 under A and 40 of 42 under B with 25 of 25 controls host-native under both. On Codex the deployment to a remote staging host selected `confirm-external-action` with `reversible-system-change` under both arms, the recorded host disagreement, and the publish control that asks to publish once and then verify the external status added `verify-completion` under B where A kept the single route; Claude Code showed no such difference.
3. Repeated trials of that publish control on Codex, alternating arms, first candidate B (description and gate sentence): A 4 of 4 single route, B 2 of 4, so B was rejected under the pre-recorded rule that the candidate's pass count on a flipped case may not fall below the current wording's. The revision B2 kept the gate unchanged and carried the description alone: the overlap case routed both routes in 4 of 4 targeted trials per host and in the full arm on each host, Claude Code 67 of 67 and Codex 65 of 67 with the deployment disagreement unchanged; the publish control under B2 kept the single route in 11 of 13 Codex trials against 8 of 8 under the current wording, within the threshold of at most two flips in thirteen that the maintainer set before the trials, and showed no flip on Claude Code. B2 is this release; the Codex over-selection rate on that control is recorded here as a known effect of the wording, with no textual link between the two sentences found.

Raw outputs stay in the maintainer's private record.
