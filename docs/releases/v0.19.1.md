# Axiom v0.19.1

Version `0.19.1` trims redundant wording in the always-loaded startup routing gate `plugin/skills/using-axiom/SKILL.md` so that the gate regains working headroom below the publication maximum. No route condition, safety rule, authorization boundary, stop condition, or pinned anchor changes; Hooks and every manifest field other than the version are unchanged. The [runtime identity](../runtime-identity.md) owns the digest and policy revision.

## Why A Patch

Version 0.19.0 left the gate at 7361 of its 7372-byte maximum, so the next route or boundary change could not land without trimming first. The [release standard](../runtime-identity.md#version-increment) classifies a size-hardening change without new capability as a patch.

## What Changed

The invocation notes, two route-once steps, the local web research preface, the `delegate-simple-task`, `confirm-external-action`, and `reversible-system-change` entries, the cross-route ownership prose, the credential-lifecycle paragraph, and the delegation-ambiguity sentence lose repeated or filler words. The `delegate-simple-task` entry keeps its three activation conditions (active gate, verified top reasoning level, prior consent) and its stop; the cross-route rules keep both independent gates and the publish-only case. Every phrase pinned by the validators, the route catalog, and the Git boundary contracts is byte-identical.

## Reduction Evidence

The gate shrinks from 7361 to 7213 UTF-8 bytes. The context-budget record below binds the reduction to the v0.19.0 surface over the same fixed workload in `evals/routing/`, with routed and no-route static contract validation passing on both surfaces. Cumulative growth against the immutable v0.7.9 baseline remains above the review threshold and stays reviewed.
