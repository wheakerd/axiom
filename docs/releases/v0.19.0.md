# Axiom v0.19.0

Version `0.19.0` adds the `verify-completion` skill. It rebuilds a completion,
progress, or success claim from current direct evidence before the claim is
made or accepted, classifies every input as observed, recalled, or summarized,
and states advice before complying when evidence conflicts with the request.
The startup routing gate gains its route line and one boundary sentence and
loses an equal amount of redundant wording; Hooks and every manifest field
other than the version and the Codex long description are unchanged. The
[runtime identity](../runtime-identity.md) owns the digest and policy revision.

## Why A Route

Long tasks and repeated context compaction let summaries stand in for
evidence. A model that continues from a compaction summary can report work as
complete that the files, commands, or remote state do not show, and a model
that follows instructions without comment can build on such a claim. Axiom's
existing routes cover parts of this: the startup gate reselects routes after
compaction, `continuous-progress` requires current evidence for supervised
runs, and `review-axiom-task` audits a task afterwards. None of them owned
the completion claim itself. The new route does, and it leaves the decision
with the host's model: the skill supplies the fact classes, the ledger, the
regrounding steps, and the advisory stance as direction, not as a script.

## What It Does

The [skill](../../plugin/skills/verify-completion/SKILL.md) freezes the
user's original criteria, re-grounds each one with a direct check, builds the
ledger in [evidence-ledger.md](../../plugin/skills/verify-completion/references/evidence-ledger.md)
(`met`, `not met`, or `unverifiable`, never "done" without an observation),
reconciles the ledger with the pending claim, and advises before complying as
[advisory-stance.md](../../plugin/skills/verify-completion/references/advisory-stance.md)
describes. After compaction, a fork, or a handoff,
[regrounding-after-compaction.md](../../plugin/skills/verify-completion/references/regrounding-after-compaction.md)
treats the summary as an index of what to check and names the failure
patterns to look for: phantom completion, goal drift, stale success, lost
constraints, and borrowed authority. Verification observes; it fixes nothing
and grants nothing.

## Routing Boundary

The route selects on explicit requests to verify or audit completion, on
resumption after compaction with unfinished mutations, and on completion
claims that rest on recalled or summarized facts. Ordinary status questions,
routine continuation, reviews of an identified Axiom task's routing and
authorization (`review-axiom-task`), and supervised multi-session progress
(`continuous-progress`) stay outside it. The routing corpus gains a
thirteenth route with canonical, paraphrased, near-miss, cross-route,
multilingual, and post-compaction cases; the benchmark profiles for both hosts
advance to their v6 generation over the v6 corpus.

## Startup Gate

The gate adds the route line and the sentence "A summary is a claim, not
evidence." to its resume and compaction boundary, and trims redundant wording
elsewhere so that it stays below the publication maximum. Every pinned
cross-route, resume, and route-anchor phrase is unchanged. The context-budget
record below measures the result against the immutable baseline.
