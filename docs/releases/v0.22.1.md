# Axiom v0.22.1

Version `0.22.1` settles five questions that the review of 0.22.0 left open, in the direction 0.22.0 set: bundled skills give direction, each host's model decides, and only authority, safety, and evidence rules stay hard. A delegated choice follows the user's environment, `clarify-intent` asks from need rather than caution, the startup gate leaves situational handling to the model within every binding rule, the documentation uses one authority wording, and the skill bodies' delegated-choice rules are pinned.

## Why A Patch

The [release standard](../runtime-identity.md#version-increment) assigns a patch increment to wording corrections and fixes without new capability. These changes reconcile `reversible-system-change` and `clarify-intent` with the delegated-choice rule that 0.22.0 introduced and state direction the model already had. No route, trigger, or protocol is added, and route matching does not widen.

## What Changed

- Startup gate (`plugin/skills/using-axiom/SKILL.md`): under an explicit delegation where every alternative is reversible and already authorized, the model chooses the alternative that fits the user's environment and goal and states which and why before acting. The Boundaries bullet adds that, within every binding rule, the model decides how to handle the request's specifics, problems that arise, deviations from the expected result, external interference, and gaps between a plan and the ideal behavior.
- `reversible-system-change`: the Authority Boundary settles an explicitly delegated target or environment choice only if every alternative is reversible and already authorized, chooses by the user's environment and goal, and states which and why before acting, instead of always asking when the environment would change execution. To stay below 8192 bytes, two unchanged lists now keep one line per item and three words ("concise", "entire", "both") were dropped without changing a rule.
- `clarify-intent`: a new scope rule asks from need, not caution. The request, context, the user's environment, and inspection may settle facts and a delegated choice whose alternatives are all reversible and already authorized, never an undelegated choice between alternatives with different route sets, write surfaces, or authority or safety boundaries; the skill asks only for a material decision they leave open, never for reassurance or as a defensive pause. The "never ... add rounds" clause that conflicted with the follow-up rule is gone, and a follow-up still needs a new material ambiguity. A delegated answer that counts is settled by the user's environment and goal, with which and why stated before acting. Two acceptance examples cover both.
- `traceable-git-submit` and the `agent-plugin-architect` route-and-trigger contract now state the pick and why before acting under a delegated choice, like the other skill bodies that accept one.
- Docs: Trust Model, Skill Reference, and Examples say "already authorized" where they said "already within your authority", "within the request's authority", or "only among reversible, already-authorized alternatives". Architecture, Trust Model, Skill Reference, Examples, and the agent-plugin-architect route contract describe environment-based delegated choices and the model's situational decisions.
- Validation: the source-contract check pins, in `traceable-git-submit`, `task-planning`, `clarify-intent`, `agent-plugin-architect` and its route-and-trigger contract, and `reversible-system-change`, each body's rule to ask or keep the choice open otherwise, the condition that every alternative is reversible and already authorized, the stated pick and why, and the offered-option limit where the body states it; `clarify-intent` also pins that the user's environment never settles an undelegated choice. How the model picks stays unpinned. The aggregate now reports 13 source-linked cross-route, resume, and delegated-choice contracts. The maintainer validation guide states that the offline choice model recognizes one literal "either ... or" pair to regression-test that boundary and neither defines nor limits how the model handles more alternatives or the situation around them.
- Review: a read-only review of the first draft found that `clarify-intent` let the user's environment settle a choice the gate leaves to the user, that `reversible-system-change` dropped "every alternative", and that the validator pinned each body's exception but not the rule to ask otherwise. All three are fixed as described above, along with three smaller wording findings. A verification of the fixes found three more small gaps, a still-wrapped changed paragraph, two ask-otherwise anchors that did not contain the ask, and an unqualified delegated choice in the new `clarify-intent` rule, and those are fixed too.

## Unchanged Boundaries

Routing still selects instructions only. A delegated choice still settles only when every alternative is reversible and already authorized; otherwise the gate selects `clarify-intent` first, and a choice the user did not delegate between alternatives with different authority stays the user's. An offered option still authorizes no persistent system change, external or remote-state change, deletion, credential use, or material cost. `delegate-simple-task` consent, the external-action authorization gate, the rollback evidence gate and its informed waiver, untrusted-input handling, and every other stop condition stay hard.
